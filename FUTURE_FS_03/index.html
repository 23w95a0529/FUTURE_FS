Roasted a few streets from where you're drinking it

Nomad started in 2019 as a single roaster in a rented garage. We now roast twice a week, in batches small enough that the person pulling your shot can tell you which farm the beans came from.

We buy direct from three growing regions, pay above fair-trade rates, and rotate our single-origin offering as new lots arrive. Nothing sits in the bag for more than three weeks before it's in your cup.

“We'd rather sell out of a good bean on a Tuesday than stretch a mediocre one through the month.”— Priya Menon, Head Roaster
